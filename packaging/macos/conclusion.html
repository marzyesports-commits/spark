Spark is installed

Ableton Live: open Settings → Plug-Ins, turn on Use VST3 Plug-in System Folders (and Use Audio Units if you prefer AU), then click Rescan. Spark appears under Plug-Ins → Spark Audio.

Logic Pro: Spark shows up under Instrument → AU Instruments → Spark Audio, and Spark FX under Audio FX.

Wavetables you export are saved to Documents → Spark → Wavetables. Audio captured by Spark FX goes to Music → Spark → Captures.
