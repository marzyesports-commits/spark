✦ SPARK

Version @VERSION@

Spark is an instrument. Drop in any sound, play it from MIDI as grains, a wavetable or the sample itself, and hit Spark to roll new variations. Shapeshift rebuilds a bounced synth note as a playable wavetable, and the FX page holds a full effects rack.

Choose Customize to pick which formats to install (VST3, Audio Unit, standalone apps).
