✦ SPARK

Version @VERSION@

This installs two plugins:

  • Spark: an instrument. Drop in any sound, play it from MIDI as grains or as a wavetable, and hit Spark to roll new variations.
  • Spark FX: the same engine as an effect, for resynthesising audio on a track.

Choose Customize to pick which formats to install (VST3, Audio Unit, standalone apps).
