✦ SPARKLEAD

Version @VERSION@

SparkLead is a lead synth: wide unison oscillators, a filter with bite, and the expression that makes leads feel played (delayed vibrato, scoops, fall-offs and glide). SparkRiff writes the lines: pick a key and a style, press Generate, hold a key to play the riff, and drag it into your DAW as MIDI.

Choose Customize to pick which formats to install (VST3, Audio Unit, standalone app).
