OBSDN is installed

Ableton Live: open Settings → Plug-Ins, turn on Use VST3 Plug-in System Folders (and Use Audio Units if you prefer AU), then click Rescan. OBSDN appears under Plug-Ins → Spark Audio.

Logic Pro: OBSDN shows up under Instrument → AU Instruments → Spark Audio.

Getting started: open the RIFF page, press Generate, then hold the key it names to hear the riff. Drag DRAG MIDI onto a MIDI track to keep it. Your presets are in Documents → Spark → Presets → OBSDN.
